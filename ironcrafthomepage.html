How It's Made

The Forge Process

From raw steel to a finished piece in your space — six stages, all completed under one roof, in this order, every time.

01

Design & Measure

We sketch the piece to your space, sit, or brand — dimensions, load, and finish confirmed before any steel is cut.

02

Precision Cutting

Galvanized steel sheet and box sections are laser and plasma cut to exact profile, ready for fabrication.

03

Welding & Fabrication

Frames are welded and ground smooth by hand, jointed for strength that carries decades of daily use.

04

Hot-Dip Galvanizing

Every weld point is coated in zinc for corrosion resistance — built for both indoor pieces and open outdoor exposure.

05

Powder Coating

Electrostatically applied and oven-cured in your chosen colour — a scratch-resistant finish, not a painted one.

06

Finishing & Install

Final assembly, hardware fitting, and quality check — then delivery and on-site installation if required.
